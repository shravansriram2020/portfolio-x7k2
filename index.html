About me

Builder, founder, and student.

I'm a senior at Agoura High School who loves building things that solve real problems. Getting a group of friends to agree on a plan was always messy — group chats got lost, rides fell through, and someone always ended up covering the bill. So I co-founded Hangouts to fix that.

What started as an idea became a shipped product: a cross-platform app on the App Store and Google Play, with natural-language conflict detection, live ETAs, ride coordination, shared photo albums, and community events.

Beyond the code, I've run the business side too — launch campaigns, promo events, release announcements, and using the app to organize community food drives.
